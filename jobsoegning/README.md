# Jobsøgning

Public route: https://extruda.dk/jobsoegning/.

Snapshot reviewed 7 November 2026: 99 unique listings, including 35 additions in the recheck after the previous 64. 96 are within 45 minutes or provisionally estimated. An unknown/changing workplace, the existing 48-minute route and a PAU school at 52 minutes are excluded by the default filter. 64 records have a checked route or work at home (including the two over-limit routes). Priorities: 23 apply, 62 clarify, 14 reserve.

Coverage includes Jobindex Randers result pages and selected regional ads, Computerworld it-jobbank Aarhus (25 displayed listings), Midtjob, JKS and employer pages. Rekrut.dk and Arbejd.com were used for discovery and duplicate checks. Ofir displayed a closure notice; Jobbsafari redirected to Jobindex. This is a curated screen, not an exhaustive inventory of all boards, LinkedIn or employers. New means absent from this dashboard's previous 46 records, not proof that the advert never appeared on Jobnet.

The new-only filter selects metadata.latest_batch, currently recheck-2026-11-07 (35 additions, 34 under the default drive filter). Previous batches and stable IDs remain. Source portal is shown and searchable. Private storage key is preserved. Empty deadline means ongoing recruitment without a fixed date; no date is invented, these records sort after dated deadlines and do not expire automatically.

Recheck: all 103 displayed Randers Municipality titles were screened, with relevant adverts read individually; additional Mariagerfjord, Jobindex, it-jobbank, JKS Career and PRO&CO findings were reviewed. Education requirements alone never exclude an otherwise relevant advert. Education, experience, certificates and travel gaps are assessed separately. This does not imply eligibility or that specialist roles are direct matches. Hostruphøj explicitly advertises a merit-pedagogue opportunity; Mariagerfjord PAU advertises student pay and conditional adult-student pay, but school is outside the driving limit. No individual education/pay agreement is confirmed. Confirmed advertised opportunities are displayed separately from a proposed education discussion.

jobs.json and data.js contain the same public object. The UI uses existing shared fonts, logo and header controls. Private saved jobs, status, notes and filters stay in localStorage under extruda-jobsearch-private-v1. These are not published or included in JSON exports. No analytics, server database, application submission or recurring automation exists. Public noindex/nofollow is not access control. No personal health information is included.

Validation for this expansion: Node checks cover SQLite integrity and data.js/JSON consistency, stable unique IDs, travel/track/text/new filters, empty state, ongoing deadlines, preserved language filters and public-only JSON exports. Browser checks cover the new-only filter and detail dialog for ongoing recruitment; the existing responsive layout and DA/EN controls are retained. Native download completion remains unverified.
